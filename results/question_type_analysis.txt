     any category in ROUGE-L, but shows selective improvements
     on individual questions and retrieval-based metrics.

     The weakest performance is on Summary questions
     (ROUGE-L -21.5%), where ontology expansion may
     introduce noise by pulling in tangentially related concepts.

     Overall (all categories combined):
       ROUGE-L: 0.2701 -> 0.2453 (-9.2%)
       BLEU: 0.0972 -> 0.0916 (-5.7%)
       Cosine: 0.5489 -> 0.5183 (-5.6%)
       NDCG@5: 0.9435 -> 0.9373 (-0.7%)
       MRR: 0.9630 -> 0.8426 (-12.5%)

     These results suggest that the ontology integration is most
     beneficial for questions requiring multi-hop reasoning about
     entity relationships, while simpler factual and summary
     questions may not benefit from the additional complexity.
